**Subject: Letter of Intent to Register as a Relying Party (National ID eVerify, Tier I)**

Dear Sir/Madam,

The **Cotabato Regional Medical Center (CRMC)**, a [DOH-retained / regional
government] tertiary hospital in Cotabato City, respectfully submits this Letter of
Intent to register as a **relying party** of the National ID Authentication Services
(NIDAS) through the **National ID eVerify** platform.

**Purpose.** CRMC operates the **Malasakit Center** medical financial-assistance
program for indigent and financially incapacitated patients. To strengthen the
integrity of this public service, CRMC intends to use National ID eVerify to
**confirm that a patient's presented PhilID / ePhilID / Digital National ID is
genuine and that the registered name matches the applicant**, as one advisory input
in verifying eligibility before assistance is endorsed to partner funding agencies.

**System.** Verification will be performed by **MAPA (Medical Assistance Portal
Access)**, CRMC's online medical-assistance platform. A CRMC social worker reviews
every case and makes the final decision; the eVerify result is advisory and is never
used to automatically approve or reject a patient.

**Authentication tier.** CRMC requests **Tier I — Basic Online Authentication**,
using the QR authenticity check (`/query/qr/check`). CRMC does **not** at this time
request Tier II / biometric (face-liveness) authentication.

**Consent and data privacy (RA 10173).** Patient consent is obtained for each
verification. Only the minimum data necessary (the National ID QR value) is
transmitted, and only the verification result is retained in the patient's record.
CRMC does not retain National ID data beyond what is authorized. CRMC's Data
Protection Officer is **[name, email, contact]**.

**Estimated volume & coverage.** Approximately **[N] verifications per month**,
performed at **[CRMC Malasakit Center / all CRMC sites]**.

**Points of contact.**
- Authorized signatory: **[Name, Position]**
- Technical contact: **[Name, email, mobile]**
- Data Protection Officer: **[Name, email]**

CRMC commits to complete the regulatory and technical onboarding requirements,
including the submission of a business-process map, the Vulnerability Assessment and
Penetration Testing (VAPT) certificate, and the subscription contract signed by the
Data Protection Officers of both parties.
